Cover what shipped, where effort went, and anything worth a look (production releases and risk flags). Use the window phrase from the user message, such as "past 24 hours" or "last 7 days".

Rules:

- Keep it under 120 words.
- Plain prose. No JSON and no bullet leaderboard.
